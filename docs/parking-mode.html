Parking modes explained

Some modes save video after an event. Others record continuously or wait in a low-power state until they're triggered.

Non-buffered parking recording

Recording starts after an event, so there's no video from before the trigger. In impact-detection modes, the G-sensor can wake the camera to start recording. These modes often use less power, but not always.

Buffered parking recording

The camera continuously watches for motion or impacts and saves video from before and after the event. Modes can detect motion, impacts, or both.

Continuous recording

Time-lapse creates sped-up video without audio. Low-bitrate recording saves continuous, normal-speed video with audio, sometimes at a reduced frame rate.

Radar can wake recording when something approaches. Hybrid parking modes switch or combine recording modes.
