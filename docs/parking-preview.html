Parking modes explained

Non-buffered recording

Recording starts after an event. There's no video from before the trigger. Low-power impact modes can sleep until an impact wakes the camera.

Buffered recording

The camera watches for supported triggers and retains video from before and after the event.

Continuous recording

Time-lapse captures spaced frames. Low-bitrate recording saves continuous video at a reduced bitrate.

Reported startup with an unconfirmed method isn't the same measurement as documented impact-to-first-frame timing. Missing values aren't zero. Ranges aren't averages.
