Includes cameras in the reviewed library with measured primary-front 3840×2160 at 30 fps driving bitrates, excluding known upscaled models such as the Rove R2-4K Dual. File dimensions alone do not prove native 4K capture. Models without those measurements and samples with an unidentified camera position are excluded. This is not a list of every 4K30 dashcam on the market.

Ranges are sorted by their upper observed endpoint, not a verified maximum-quality setting. T340 rows use the reviewed, configuration-specific front-camera measurements rather than mixed-quality scan ranges. Other quality settings remain unknown unless captured. Codecs, scenes, HDR and connected-camera counts differ; bitrate alone is not an image-quality ranking.

Additional 4K models not yet ranked

These known models are not silently omitted or assigned borrowed values. They need a comparable front-camera, driving-only video bitrate at 4K30. This covers our reviewed library and research, not every dashcam on the market.

  • BlackVue DR970X LTE Plus: Profile retains approximately 61 Mbps 4K30 front video metadata, but not its recording-mode linkage. Needs driving-only confirmation before ranking; do not borrow the non-LTE value. Evidence
  • BlackVue DR970X Box-2CH Plus: Official 4K model reference; no measured front 4K30 driving bitrate retained. Do not borrow a sibling model's rate. Evidence
  • BlackVue Elite 10: Submitted card structure has no measured media specifications. Official front 4K30 capability does not establish measured bitrate. Evidence
  • BlackVue DR900S-2CH: 4K30 capability is documented, but retained measured driving clips are 1080p. Manufacturer bitrate ranges are not measured 4K30 samples. Evidence
  • Thinkware ARC 900: Official manual documents 4K30; no measured front driving bitrate retained. Evidence
  • Thinkware U1000: Retained 4K30 research reports a complete-file storage rate. A video-track-only rate is needed for this video-bitrate chart. Evidence
  • Nextbase 622GW: Official 4K30 capability; no comparable measured driving video-track bitrate retained. Evidence
  • VIOFO A329: Existing research retains a 4K60 sample, not a comparable 4K30 video-track measurement. Do not reuse A329S or A329T rates. Evidence
  • Vantrue X4: Retained 3840x2160 at 29.97 fps research reports complete-file storage rates. A separate video-track rate is needed. Evidence
