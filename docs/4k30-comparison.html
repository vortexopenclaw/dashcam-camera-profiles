Measured driving video, not advertised maximums. Audio excluded. Settings, codecs and measurement notes · PNG · SVG · CSV

Includes cameras in the reviewed library with measured primary-front 3840×2160 at 30 fps driving bitrates, excluding known upscaled models such as the Rove R2-4K Dual. File dimensions alone do not prove native 4K capture. Models without those measurements and samples with an unidentified camera position are excluded. This is not a list of every 4K30 dashcam on the market.

Ranges are sorted by their upper observed endpoint, not a verified maximum-quality setting. T340 rows use the reviewed, configuration-specific front-camera measurements rather than mixed-quality scan ranges. Other quality settings remain unknown unless captured. Codecs, scenes, HDR and connected-camera counts differ; bitrate alone is not an image-quality ranking.
