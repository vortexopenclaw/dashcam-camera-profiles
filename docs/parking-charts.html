Read the comparison in context

Current comparisons require one known measurement voltage. Watts can compare sources at different voltages, but adapter losses and the measurement boundary can differ.

Recording delay after impact detection and impact to the first recorded frame are separate methods. Unknown startup methods are not plotted as either metric. Missing data is excluded, never treated as zero.

A range shows only its measured endpoints, not an average. A zero measurement stays at the baseline. Source precision is retained, and power marked approximately was derived in the source dataset.
