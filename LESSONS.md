# Lessons

## 2026-08-13 Keep the reference separate from the offloader

- The community repository is the canonical technical database and public browser.
- Dashcam Offloader may consume a pinned release, but its user interface should remain focused on card detection and offloading.
- Do not hide community research inside an application-specific model or duplicate it manually across frontends.

## 2026-08-13 Preserve evidence boundaries

- A manual documents capability; a real card proves observed storage behavior.
- Driving and parking measurements must retain their recording mode instead of being flattened into one camera specification.
- Missing bitrate, filename, folder, or manual evidence must remain visibly missing.
